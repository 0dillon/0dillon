# Research Interests & Explorations

Notes and ongoing research at the intersection of AI, cryptographic primitives, and decentralized finance:

## Autonomous Agents & Safety
- **Bounded Agency:** Defining strict behavioral bounds and pre-execution assertion checks for LLM-driven agents executing transactions on public distributed ledgers.
- **Auditable Intent:** Structuring deterministic event logs and verifiable execution traces so agent actions can be audited post-facto.

## Cryptographic Verifiability
- **Verifiable Delay Functions (VDF):** Incorporating Wesolowski VDF proofs for fair, unbiasable time-delayed dispute resolution without reliance on centralized sequencers.
- **Merkle Trees & Receipt Anchoring:** Optimizing cryptographic accumulators for gas-efficient batch proofs on constrained execution environments.
